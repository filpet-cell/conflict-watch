About the data
  • News is aggregated every 5 minutes from open RSS feeds of BBC News, Al Jazeera, The Guardian, UN News, Deutsche Welle, France 24 and ReliefWeb (UN OCHA).
  • Only items with direct security, military, diplomatic or humanitarian relevance to a conflict are shown — general news that merely mentions a country is filtered out.
  • A conflict is marked corroborated only when 2+ independent sources report on it within 48 hours. Single-source items remain visible but unbadged.
  • Active, potential and civil unrest designations are editorially curated, guided by the CFR Global Conflict Tracker, Crisis Group CrisisWatch and ACLED — the linked assessments are the live authority.
  • Linked entities are limited to what public sanctions lists (OFAC/EU/UK/UN) and official government records already document. Follow the source link on each entry to verify.
  • Auto-detected unrest pins (dashed) are machine-suggested when unrest-related reporting appears for a country without a curated entry: blue = one source so far (unconfirmed), green = corroborated by 2+ independent sources. They are suggestions, not confirmed assessments.
  • Interface translations into 9 languages are human-curated; in all other offered languages the interface stays in English. News articles in any non-English language open via machine translation, which is imperfect — the original article is always available through the source name.
  • Live-coverage links point to external OSINT maps and broadcaster streams; their content is not verified by this app. This app is an aggregator, not a substitute for official guidance.
